Selected projects

Robotics · CV

360° Fisheye Guiding Waistband

A vision-impairment guiding waistband equipped with a 360° fisheye panoramic camera. Achieves full-surround environmental perception and delivers obstacle warnings via haptic vibration feedback.

View project →

Research · Embedded

OmniBelt — Assistive Navigation Belt

Raspberry Pi 5 belt with 12 vibration motors. Fast loop runs YOLO11n-seg segmentation, slow loop queries a VLM, mapping 12 sectors of haptic feedback for real-time guidance.

View project →

Startup · AR

Libereye — AR Glasses Navigation

Turn-by-turn navigation for blind users fusing VLM vision, TOF ranging and a BLE haptic wristband. Reverse-engineered wearable protocol; deployed on uni-app Vue3.

View project →

Research · Depth

Radius-aware Fisheye Depth Estimation

A lightweight 836K-parameter U-Net with radius-aware fisheye PSF, improving outer-ring RMSE by ~41%. INT8 model compressed to 3.03 MB for on-device inference.
